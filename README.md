## What does “cash back” mean?

Cash back is money returned to you after you make an eligible purchase through a participating retailer.

For example, suppose a store offers 5% cash back and you place an eligible $100 order. The potential cash back would be $5, subject to the store’s terms, exclusions, maximum limits, and final transaction confirmation.

You normally pay the retailer’s regular price at checkout. ShopBack receives a referral commission from the retailer or affiliate network, then shares part of that commission with the shopper. The cash back is not usually an instant discount applied to the checkout total. It first appears as pending and becomes available only after the merchant confirms that the order is valid and will not be returned or canceled.

That distinction matters. A cash back rate shown before you shop is a potential earning rate, not an unconditional promise that every dollar in the order will qualify.

Common reasons the final amount can differ include:

- Some products or categories may be excluded.
- Taxes, shipping, service fees, or gift cards may not qualify.
- The offer may apply only to new customers.
- A cash back rate may have a maximum payout.
- Returns, cancellations, exchanges, and partial refunds can reduce or remove the reward.
- Merchant-specific coupon codes may invalidate the transaction.
- The offer may be available only through the app, website, or browser extension.

The rate and terms shown on the retailer’s ShopBack page should therefore be checked immediately before checkout.

Sometimes. ShopBack’s US guidance explains that cash back may be combined with retailer promo codes and credit card rewards because these benefits can come from different parts of the transaction.

A typical stacking strategy might look like this:

1. Activate an eligible ShopBack offer.
2. Apply a retailer coupon that the offer terms allow.

The key phrase is **that the offer terms allow**. A merchant may exclude orders using unapproved codes or special promotions. Some retailers also calculate cash back only on the discounted merchandise subtotal.

The correct order of operations is to check the ShopBack terms first, then apply only discounts that are compatible with the offer.
